Cultural Programme Producer at Nordhamn City Museum

Nordhamn City Museum is looking for a cultural programme producer to help develop the museum's public activities.
This is a permanent, full-time position. The deadline for applications is 30 November.

About the role
You will produce exhibitions and public programmes for all ages and be responsible for budget and follow-up.
You will apply for grants and run projects from idea to evaluation. You will work in partnership with civil society,
associations and schools, and work actively with accessibility so that more people can take part in our programme.

You have
- experience of producing public programmes and exhibitions
- budget responsibility and experience of applying for grants
- experience of partnership work with associations, schools and other organisations
- good knowledge of accessibility
- excellent command of Swedish and English

Desirable
- pedagogical experience, for example guided tours or school programmes
- experience of digital formats and VR in exhibitions

About us
The museum is part of Nordhamn's cultural administration and welcomes about 80,000 visitors a year.
